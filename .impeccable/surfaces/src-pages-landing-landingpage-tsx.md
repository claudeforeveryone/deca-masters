# Landing surface brief (signed-out /)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: high school DECA members (often minors, phone first) deciding whether to make an account.
Action: "Create an account" (primary) or "Sign in". Proof: DECA's real format facts and the real event list from
content/events.json; the tools shown working with labelled illustrative values. Constraints: no fake activity or
counts, no AI-written questions or terms, independent of DECA Inc., no DECA logo, corners 2-6px.
Full design doc: hq/design/DECA_TERMINAL.md (Omega HQ).
